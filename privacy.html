Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 24, 2026

At Rendercept, we specialize in high-performance web development, custom static architectures, and scalable web engineering for tech companies and enterprises. We are dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and security of all personal and project data shared with us.

01. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, request a consultation, or partner with us for web development projects, we may collect the following types of information:

Contact Information

Your name, work email address, company name, phone number, and related business contact details provided through our contact forms or direct communications.

Project & Technical Details

Project scopes, tech stack requirements, architectural blueprints, repository references, wireframes, and business goals submitted during proposal inquiries or scoping calls.

Usage & Technical Telemetry

Non-identifying technical metadata including browser type, operating system, referral sources, visit timestamps, and anonymous interaction metrics used to evaluate and optimize our site performance.

02. How We Use Your Information

Rendercept uses collected information strictly for professional and operational purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To design, build, test, and deploy custom web solutions, technical architectures, and software engineering deliverables tailored to your business needs.
  • Proposal & Inquiry Response: To evaluate project specifications, generate accurate engineering estimates, and respond to your communication requests.
  • Client Communications: To share technical documentation, milestone progress updates, deployment notifications, and invoicing details.
  • Infrastructure & Security: To protect our digital assets, prevent malicious activities, enforce contractual agreements, and maintain high availability of our systems.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or proprietary project information to third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

04. Your Rights & Data Retention

You retain full authority over your personal information. Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request rectification or updates to any incomplete or inaccurate data.
  • Request permanent deletion or purging of your records from our systems, subject to legal and accounting obligations.
  • Withdraw consent for optional communications at any time.
